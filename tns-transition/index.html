Fresh Green Light is joining The Next Street

After 17 years and more than 35,000 new drivers, we're retiring and handing the keys to The Next Street: a family-run Connecticut driving school that started the same year we did and grew into the state's leader in driver's education. The transition happens in mid-December. For you, nothing changes: you'll get everything you paid for, and you'll continue to see many familiar faces.

Common Questions

Will the lessons and classes I've paid for be honored?

Yes, every one: lessons and classes you've already taken, ones you've scheduled and ones you still need to book.

Do I need to do anything right now?

No. Keep booking the way you do now.

Is my road test date still on?

Yes. Booked dates stand.

Will I keep my instructor?

You'll continue to see many familiar faces. We can't promise a specific instructor.

Can I still sign up?

Yes. Anything you buy is honored after the transition.

What happens in mid-December?

Before the transition, we'll email every family a short guide covering what changes and when: dates, logging in, booking and payments.
